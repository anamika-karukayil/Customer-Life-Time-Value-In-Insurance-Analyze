# Customer-Life-Time-Value-In-Insurance-Analyze
## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer lifetime value (CLV) in the insurance domain using an Excel dashboard. The objective is to evaluate customer behavior, premium contribution, claim patterns, and policy performance to support data-driven decision making.

## Business Problem

Insurance companies face challenges in identifying high-value customers, managing claim risks, and optimizing policy strategies. This project helps analyze customer data to understand revenue contribution and claim trends.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains the following features:

* policy id – Unique identifier for each policy
* customer name – Name of the customer
* gender – Customer gender
* age – Customer age
* policy type – Type of insurance policy
* policy startdate – Policy start date
* policy enddate – Policy end date
* premium amount – Amount paid as premium
* claim amount – Amount claimed by the customer
* claim status – Approved, Pending, or Rejected
* region – Customer region
* sales agent – Assigned agent
* month – Month of transaction
* year – Year of transaction

## Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Excel

## Dashboard Features

* Total Premium vs Total Claim Amount
* Total Policies and Customers
* Average Premium Analysis
* Claim Approval Rate
* Claim Status Breakdown
* Region-wise Policy Distribution
* Monthly Premium Trend
* Policy Type Distribution
* Claims Trend by Year
* Sales Agent Performance
* Interactive Filters for better analysis

## Key Insights

* Approved claims dominate overall claim distribution.
* Certain regions contribute more to premium revenue.
* Premium trends vary across months and years.
* Policy types show different risk and revenue patterns.
